Record versioning and recovery

Run these scripts only on a fresh database; remapping accounts changes which ledger accounts the reconciliation compares.

Costing method. I chose perpetual weighted-average cost per product and warehouse. I picked it because FIFO needs cost layers and standard cost needs variance upkeep. LIFO isn't allowed under IFRS. The reasoning and alternatives are in docs/ACCOUNTING_TREATMENT.md. It's a recommendation that your accountant needs to confirm. Changing method later means revaluing history.

Landed costs and ledger postings

Finance users enter landed costs (freight, duty) on a goods receipt. They are allocated across lines by value, and the shares always add up exactly.
Receipts, dispatches, customer and supplier returns, and stock adjustments now post to the ledger. Receipts debit Inventory and credit Goods received not invoiced. Dispatches debit Cost of sales and credit Inventory.
This switches on only once four accounts are mapped: inventory, cost of sales, goods received not invoiced, and inventory adjustment. Until then stock stays quantity-only, as before. A fifth account, purchase price variance, is mapped separately.
Stock received before valuation was switched on can't be issued until it gets an opening valuation. The offset goes to the inventory adjustment account and should be reviewed by an accountant.
Each stock pool holds a single currency. Receiving in another currency while stock is on hand is refused.
Purchasing controls

Admins set an approval threshold. Orders at or above it, and any order in a non-default currency, wait for a finance user other than the creator before goods can be received. Rejection needs a comment, and decisions are kept in an append-only history.
A vendor bill can be linked to a purchase order. It's matched against the value of goods received, using order prices, plus landed costs. A larger bill needs an override reason and is stored as an "exception".
Matched bills clear the Goods received not invoiced accrual, and any excess is expensed as purchase price variance.
The sub-ledger reconciliation now also compares stock value and the accrual with their ledger accounts.
UI. The Inventory page has a Valuation tab for finance users: stock value, opening valuation, and the approval threshold for admins. Purchase orders get Approve and Reject buttons. The goods receipt form has a landed-cost field. The Finance bill form has a purchase-order selector, and the account mappings include the five new accounts.

Tax and sign-offs

Under Settings → Jurisdiction, an administrator records, per country and scope (tax or numbering), the adviser's name, firm, the opinion they relied on, the date, and for tax the rounding method they confirmed. Sign-offs can be revoked with a reason.
Until a sign-off exists for the organisation's country, invoices behave exactly as before: no tax, and ID-based numbers.
The sign-off only stores the claim; the application cannot check it.
Once tax is on, tax is computed on the server in integer minor units. Two rounding methods are available: once per rate, or per line.
Each invoice stores its tax breakdown. Output tax posts to a new tax-payable account.
The reference rates (LV 21%, LT 21%, EE 24%, DE 19%, GB 20%) are unverified starting values for your adviser to confirm.
Numbering and corrections

With a numbering sign-off, invoices and credit notes get gapless numbers from a locked counter, for example INV-LV-2026-000001. A failed issue consumes no number.
I ran eight parallel issues on PostgreSQL, including the first issue of a year. The numbers came out consecutive with no gaps or duplicates.
Issued invoices are never edited. Credit notes are the correction path.
A credit note can't exceed what is still owed, and can't exceed the net invoiced per tax code. Refunding money already received isn't supported.
Invoice and credit-note PDFs now show frozen seller and customer details, the tax breakdown and payment instructions. Issuing needs the organisation's legal name and address once either sign-off is on.
Bank workflow

I chose provider-agnostic statement import, camt.053 XML and CSV, because it needs no bank credentials and works across all five countries.
Lines are matched by exact amount and currency to recorded receipts and vendor payments. A matching reference in the bank text lets auto-match pair them.
Lines with no book record, such as bank fees, are ignored with a recorded reason.
A period report lists unmatched items on both sides.
Re-importing a file, or importing overlapping statements, doesn't duplicate transactions.
A statement whose balances don't add up is rejected, as is XML with entity declarations.
Nothing connects to a bank or posts to the ledger from here.
Foreign exchange. Left out on purpose. Everything stays single-currency. The decision and what would trigger it are in docs/ACCOUNTING_TREATMENT.md.
