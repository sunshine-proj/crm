Finance role

There are now three roles: ROLE_USER, ROLE_FINANCE, and ROLE_ADMIN, which includes finance.
Finance users get the ledger, payables and reports, invoice issue, customer payments, reimbursements, profitability and cost visibility.
Admins alone configure accounts, mappings and periods, post opening balances and reversals, manage cost rates and stock adjustments, edit clients and settings, and restore records.
Grant roles with php bin/console app:user:roles <email> ROLE_FINANCE.
HTTP-level allow/deny tests check each role.
The Finance menu shows for finance and admin users.
Record versioning and recovery

Creates, updates and deletes of clients, contacts, projects, milestones, tasks, products, suppliers and warehouses go into an append-only record_revision table (new migration).
Admins can list revisions, roll a record back, or recreate a deleted one. A recreated record gets a new ID.
Settings has a new "Record history" panel for this.
Rows removed by database cascades, such as a client's projects, are not captured.
Invoices, journals, payments and stock movements are deliberately excluded, since they are corrected through their own workflows.
I checked it over HTTP on PostgreSQL: update, rollback, and delete-then-recreate all worked.

What now exists

Client account details: clients have legal name, registration and VAT numbers, country, billing address and e-mail, website, industry, and an account status. They are searchable and filterable. Registration and VAT numbers, billing address and billing e-mail are only returned to finance users and admins, and non-finance users can't find clients by those fields.
Detail pages: #/clients/<id> and #/projects/<id> combine the profile, contacts, activity, notes, deals, projects, tasks, milestones, time, expenses, invoices, budget, schedule and change history. The project page also has a period-filtered financial report for finance users.
Task board: Project Management has a Timetable / Task board toggle. Cards move by drag-and-drop or a select, and a move is rolled back if the API rejects it.
Deals: a new Deals section with a pipeline board from lead to won/lost. A lost deal needs a reason. There is an append-only stage history and a forecast using fixed stage probabilities, per currency.
Reminders: php bin/console app:reminders:run creates in-app due-soon, overdue and escalation notifications, and a bell in the header shows them. The new scheduler compose service runs the command hourly. Each user sets their own lead days, escalation threshold and on/off switch. Overdue work escalates to the project manager, or to admins if the assignee is the manager.
Change history: deals are now versioned. Any signed-in user can see who changed a record and which fields, without the values. Admins see values and can restore.
Time-entry rules: the author can withdraw pending or rejected time that hasn't been invoiced. A manager or admin can reopen approved, uninvoiced time with a reason. Invoiced time is locked. Decisions and reopenings are kept in an append-only history.
Project profitability: accepts a from/to period.
AR/AP reconciliation: a new /api/finance/reports/subledger-reconciliation endpoint compares sub-ledgers with their journal postings and with the mapped AR/AP accounts. It reports missing, differing and orphaned postings. It's shown on the Receivables & payables tab.
Accounting treatment: docs/ACCOUNTING_TREATMENT.md writes down what the ledger does for sales and inventory. A test checks that stock movements create no journal entries.
Not done

E-mail reminders: I didn't add symfony/mailer because it is blocked by security advisories on symfony/mime. Delivery is in-app only.
Bank-statement reconciliation: no bank workflow has been chosen. Manual journals on the AR/AP accounts will show up as differences in the reconciliation report.
Accounting treatment: it is defined but not validated by an accountant. It has no inventory asset, no cost of sales and no credit notes, and it needs a costing method first.
Smaller gaps:
won deals don't convert to quotes or projects
invoiced time can't be corrected without credit notes
the detail pages load whole lists and need pagination for large data
